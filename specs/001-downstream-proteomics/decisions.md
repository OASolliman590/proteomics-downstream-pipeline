| D-43 | PERMANOVA exact permutation accounting (review follow-up) | Every test records `n_admissible_permutations` N (distinct admissible relabellings of its scheme: all observations, whole subjects, within subjects, or design rows within groups for covariate terms), `p_min_attainable` = S/N (S = group-name permutations that keep every block's label multiset, which leave F unchanged; S = 1 for covariate terms) and `permutation_enumeration`. | D-46 | Sensitivity models (R14b) | A covariate-adjusted, subgroup or interaction model is a named secondary model whose design is re-planned through `design_service.replan` (exact rank and estimability, re-verified with `design_rank` in R), with SM04 coverage and featurewise estimability re-applied on its observations and the primary engine settings (limma, trend, robust, frozen consensus correlation). | D-47 | Phenotype association (R14c) | The phenotype enters a re-planned design as a centred continuous term (ordinal phenotypes as scores 1..L of their declared levels); its coefficient is tested with the primary limma settings in family `phenotype__<column>` (`__within_<group>` for within-group analyses), BH. Complete-case units per phenotype; at least 4 units and a non-constant phenotype are required (E_PHENOTYPE_SMALL_N). "Aliased with group" means recorded in one group only or determined by group/covariates (exact rank deficiency); pooled analysis is then refused (E_PHENOTYPE_ALIASED) and a required module rejects the plan. Non-overlapping group ranges are a warning (W_PHENOTYPE_GROUP_SEPARATED), not a refusal, so a Simpson pattern stays visible. Pooled rows carry descriptive within-group OLS slopes and a Simpson flag (all within-group slopes share a sign opposite to the pooled effect). The correlation method uses features observed in every analysed unit, permutes phenotype values over units (whole subjects when constant within subject, else within subject) from one L'Ecuyer stream, and the partial correlation permutes the residualised phenotype. Any phenotype imputation request is refused (E_PHENOTYPE_IMPUTATION). |
| D-48 | Biomarker evaluation (R14d) | Positive class = the contrast's first required group. Units are biological units (subjects in subject-blocked designs); folds are dealt per unit class ("0", "1", or "mixed" when the class varies within a subject) after shuffling, continuing the deal across classes. The candidate universe is the set of features genuinely observed in every analysed observation (a label-free planning restriction like SM26; with `imputation: train_median` at least half observed, medians fitted in-fold). Every learned step is fitted on the training fold: medians, non-constant filter, centring/scaling, selection (top-k by |AUC − 0.5| or moderated t, or the nonzero set of an elastic-net/lasso path), tuning (inner stratified grouped CV; maximum inner AUC, ties broken by inner Brier score for probabilistic classifiers, then grid order), classifier and Youden threshold (on the inner out-of-fold scores). Penalised logistic is ridge glmnet on the selected features (lambda path from the training fold unless fixed); SVM uses decision values; scores are oriented by the fitted model's class coding. RNG: one L'Ecuyer stream per job (0 nested CV, 1..B permutations, B+1 threshold bootstrap, then fixed panels and the locked model), so results do not depend on the number of workers. Single-feature AUCs use unit means and are labelled `in_sample` (auto direction flagged direction-optimistic); paired designs refuse them (E_BIOMARKER_PAIRED_AUC). The claim audit forbids "diagnostic", "clinical utility" and "validated" in outputs except the label tokens and the mandated phrase "not externally validated". The compute guard uses a reference cost model calibrated on the 8-core reference Mac (0.02 s per fit plus 2e-6 s per training cell, divided by threads); with the contract defaults (100 repeats, B = 1000) a 2000-feature study is refused, never silently reduced. The locked model hash is the SHA-256 of `locked_model.json` (features, medians, centring, scaling, classifier coefficients or serialized model hash, threshold). |
| D-49 | Co-abundance and networks (R14e) | An enabled `post_de.networks` block must declare `coabundance` or `ppi` (else E_NETWORKS_NOT_DECLARED); nothing runs by default. The soft threshold is the smallest candidate (1-10, 12-30 by 2) whose scale-free-topology fit index (the pickSoftThreshold definition: 10 equal-width connectivity bins, −sign(slope)·R² of log10 frequency on log10 mean connectivity) reaches 0.8; when none does, the published signed-network default by sample size (n < 20: 18, < 30: 16, < 40: 14, else 12) is used with a warning. Dendrograms (average linkage) are cut by dynamicTreeCut without the PAM stage at a declared cut height (default 0.9 TOM dissimilarity, 0.5 for 1 − r), so unrelated features stay unassigned. Stability: units resampled with replacement, parameters fixed, best Jaccard per module. Module-trait tests fit limma on eigengenes in family `module_trait__<trait>`, labelled `module_level`, with the primary robust setting and trend = FALSE (eigengenes are centred, so their means are identically 0 and a mean-variance trend is undefined; recorded in the `moderation` column); a single module is tested like several (A-2026-10-01-19).
