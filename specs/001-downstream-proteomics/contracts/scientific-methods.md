# Scientific analysis contract v1.0.0

Normative rules apply to maintained analysis. They do not retroactively change the archived pipeline. Sources and API qualification are consolidated in research.md and scientific-methods-research.md. A user can choose a prespecified eligible alternative; changing the primary method after seeing results creates an explicitly exploratory reanalysis.

## 1. Measurement and sample identity

**Boundary:** one row represents a quantified protein/protein group; columns represent declared observations. Feature IDs are stable opaque identifiers. Accession, gene symbol and description are annotations, not interchangeable row keys. Protein groups retain all constituent accessions and ambiguity flags. Decoy, contaminant and reverse-search flags come from source metadata; their absence is unknown, not proof of removal.

**Biological unit:** sample metadata records biological_unit_id, observation_id, technical_replicate_id, subject_id for repeated measurements, group/covariates, assay_batch, acquisition_run, multiplex_id/channel and reference-channel status as applicable. Biological replicate n is counted after technical handling. Four injections of one rat never become n=4. Technical replicate aggregation is explicit: arithmetic mean of declared linear abundance or arithmetic mean of log2 abundance have different estimands and must be named; choose the scale-appropriate rule in the plan and propagate coverage. Do not average P values or already fitted coefficients as a substitute for model fitting.

**Minimum design:** descriptive QC is allowed with n=1. Ordinary inferential group contrasts require at least two independent biological units per relevant group and positive residual degrees of freedom for the feature/design; n=2 is permitted only with prominent small-n limitation, not a claim of reliable power. Recommended experimental replication cannot be enforced retrospectively. For ordinary available-case limma/DEqMS, a feature entirely missing in a target group is nonestimable. Eligible proDA may model this case; report its actual observation counts, prior dependence and native uncertainty rather than inventing zeros or borrowing available-case inference. Entirely unobserved features across the study are excluded. Technical aggregation and engine-specific missingness policy precede model n accounting.

**Source scale:** accepted states linear_positive, log2, ratio_log2, or standardized_unknown. Only known positive linear quantities are log2 transformed. A zero log2 abundance is a valid observed zero, not missing. Linear zeros are missing only under an explicit source encoding contract; negative linear values otherwise fail. Ratios are not absolute abundance. Standardized/unknown matrices may receive descriptive QC, but abundance effect interpretation/inference requires a scientifically documented scale. Preserve a missing mask and any known prior-imputation mask separately from numeric values.

## 2. Intake profiles

Mandatory profiles are canonical wide matrix, canonical long table, the archived workbook importer, and declarative column-mapping intake for validated vendor tables. Supply tested mappings for representative pinned/versioned synthetic DIA-NN protein, MaxQuant proteinGroups, FragPipe/MSstats-style protein abundance, and Spectronaut protein exports. Each mapping must define source granularity and quantify what information it does/does not reconstruct. Do not claim support for every vendor version. Source peptide/precursor tables cannot be silently collapsed as if they were already protein abundance.

Supported assays are protein-level LFQ DDA, LFQ DIA, TMT multiplex and processed protein abundances with declared upstream state. The maintained engine must reject phosphosite/localization and single-cell profiles with an explicit unsupported-scope message. A custom protein-level mapping remains available when a vendor schema is not recognized; mapping fields are validated rather than guessed from similar names.

## 3. Preprocessing and missingness

Default for already processed log2 abundance is **preserve input**. It is scientifically preferable to report missing normalization provenance than to normalize twice. For unnormalized eligible log2 LFQ, explicit median centering is supported and assumes most measured proteins are stable or that the measured distribution represents a suitable scaling reference. Quantile normalization is an explicit sensitivity option with its assumptions; it is not the default for dropout models because missingness can distort distribution matching. Reference-protein/internal-standard normalization requires a declared reference set and adequate coverage.

For TMT, use explicit within-plex loading normalization. Cross-plex analysis declares either a bridge/reference-channel strategy, or an identifiable no-bridge design estimating replicated within-plex effects with a plex covariate. Check required bridge channels for bridge mode and design/nonconfounding for either mode; a bridge is not universally necessary. Do not invent a bridge or treat channels within unrelated plexes as directly comparable by name. Save normalization factors, pre/post diagnostics, upstream correction status and resulting estimand; ratio compression cannot be solved merely by this downstream pipeline. With supplied upstream normalization, preserve it by default.

Primary limma introduces no new imputation. If input was imputed upstream, it cannot be called entirely observed; coverage uses the original observation mask where available, otherwise observed coverage is unknown and only numeric availability can be described. Missingness is summarized by observation, group, feature and abundance, including prior imputation. Mechanism labels MCAR/MAR/MNAR are assumptions or model diagnostics, not observable facts assigned by a single missing-rate cutoff. Coverage policy is prespecified and contrast/engine-specific: ordinary available-case contrasts default to fraction≥0.5 and at least two observed biological units per required group, with residual-df/estimability checks. Qualified proDA uses its documented dropout eligibility rather than automatically discarding every all-missing target group. More stringent settings are supported; record every exclusion and tested universe. Presence/absence contrasts are a separate declared exploratory detection endpoint (two-sided Fisher exact for independent two-group binary detection, BH in its own family); they require a genuine observation mask, are not continuous abundance rescue and are inapplicable to paired/complex designs until an eligible backend is implemented.

Sensitivity methods may include deterministic minimum replacement, explicitly stochastic left-shifted Gaussian replacement, KNN and complete-case fits. Name the actual algorithm; “MinDet” cannot describe random draws. Seeds, distribution parameters, donor scope and imputed cells are recorded. These sensitivity results cannot replace the primary analysis automatically. No label-dependent imputation or parameter tuning to increase discoveries. Imputation uncertainty is not erased by treating invented values as independent observations; reports state limitations. PCA may use row-median replacement in its own display matrix and must never feed that matrix into primary inference.

Batch covariates enter the design when supplied and identifiable. Descriptive batch-adjusted PCA may be provided using a documented method with preservation of biological design, but the original model data remain the primary inference input. Do not remove batch then adjust the same effect again unless an explicitly justified model calls for it. Fully confounded batch/treatment is a design limitation, not a preprocessing problem to “correct away”.

## 4. QC and observation policy

Mandatory QC includes input/retained dimensions, biological versus technical n, missingness, intensity distributions, normalization factors, pairwise correlations with coverage, PCA explained variance and sample scores, hierarchical relationships where informative, and the design matrix/alias report. PCA centering/scaling and display imputation are explicit. All-identical features, nonfinite input and too few observations result in valid explanatory output, not crashed plotting or fake variance.

Outlier metrics are diagnostic. No sample is removed solely because it separates on PCA or weakens significance. Configured exclusions require a reason and are present in the frozen plan; post-QC exclusion produces a new sensitivity run. Influence analysis reports leave-one-biological-unit effects and intervals for all prespecified primary contrasts. Omit all repeated/technical observations of that unit together where applicable. Aggregate stability only over relevant omissions and label global versus relevant-group summaries. Use effect changes/significance sensitivity descriptively; do not pretend selected-feature leave-one-out is held-out biological validation.

## 5. Design, contrasts and exactness

Supported designs: independent group means, additive covariates/batches, explicit group interactions, paired subjects via appropriate fixed blocking when estimable, and repeated measurements via limma duplicateCorrelation with a declared subject block. A finite grammar generates R design/contrast matrices. Allow categorical/continuous terms and interactions; disallow arbitrary R function calls, filesystem expressions and arbitrary eval. Center continuous covariates using frozen parameters; specify reference levels and record the evaluated matrices. Do not automatically categorize continuous covariates.

Before fit: require exact metadata/matrix alignment, finite covariates or an explicit exclusion policy, no rank deficiency, sufficient units and positive residual df; test whether each contrast lies in the estimable row space. During fit: recheck featurewise design/df when observations are missing. Record exclusion/nonestimability reasons per feature and contrast rather than silently shrinking a table.

**Critical standard-error rule:** limma `contrasts.fit` can have approximate unscaled SEs with a nonorthogonal design and missing values or precision weights. The adapter must use contrast-as-coefficient reparametrization/refitting, or a rigorously equivalent exact featurewise covariance calculation verified against a direct fit. One-way group means without blocking may use its exact contrasts shortcut. Do not assume a batch-adjusted or weighted model is covered by the shortcut. Test sparse, weighted, nonorthogonal and blocked cases independently. [limma contrasts.fit documentation](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bioc/limma/master/man/contrasts.fit.Rd).

For duplicateCorrelation, preserve block/correlation inputs and record estimated consensus correlation and method/version. No assumption that robust eBayes makes regression robust to bad samples. Missing block IDs or inadequate replication block this mode. More complex multiple-random-effect/dream models are not exposed in this release; unsupported formulas must fail rather than masquerade as supported repeated-measures analysis.

## 6. Differential engines and applicability

| Engine | Allowed use | Required evidence | Rejected/inapplicable cases |
|---|---|---|---|
| limma | Declared protein-level log2 abundance, identifiable linear design; independent/paired/eligible repeated data | Input/preprocessing state, observation hierarchy, exact contrasts, residual df; trend/robust settings in plan | Unknown scale, fake biological replication, aliased effects, all missing target group |
| DEqMS | Count-aware variance modeling when valid protein-level peptide/PSM support counts accompany eligible abundance | Nonnegative/positive count semantics, matching feature IDs, grain and aggregation rule, acquisition context and count provenance | Invented count from observed samples, arbitrary abundance proxy, unsupported blocks/weights, counts mismatched to protein group |
| proDA | LFQ protein log2 data with missingness represented as dropout and no incompatible prior imputation | Actual missing mask, LFQ assay and upstream imputation/normalization state; eligible independent fixed-effects design | TMT by default, preimputed input without original missing information, repeated random-effects/block design, unsupported threshold-null claim |

All three adapters are required software deliverables. Study-level selection is explicit. Unsupported input-method combinations fail before the backend runs; no fallback to limma after proDA/DEqMS failure. Methods are not ranked by hit count. Each result carries method/version/model ID and its actual hypothesis.

**Limma:** `lmFit` and eligible exact contrasts; empirical Bayes trend and robust enabled by documented default with statmod available, diagnostics for trend and prior/posterior variance, and optional declared alternative settings. A zero-null test uses moderated inference; when the biological question is whether |effect| exceeds a positive declared margin, use TREAT with that margin, not zero-null BH plus an observed effect cutoff. The output can include both hypotheses if requested, with distinct testing families and an explicit primary one. Explanatory display cutoffs remain separate from inference. Report model-appropriate effect CI, moderated SE/df where applicable, and mean abundance.

**DEqMS:** follow the installed official sequence: limma fit/contrasts/eBayes, assign a feature-aligned validated `fit$count`, then spectraCounteBayes and outputResult for the target coefficient. Actual `fit$count` values MUST be finite and strictly positive after any declared adjustment. Input unique+razor peptide counts and PSM counts are not interchangeable. An explicit scientifically justified aggregation/pseudocount policy is required for zero/multiple sample counts; no hidden +1. Preserve original zero counts in provenance; missing counts are never converted to zero. Preserve original limma and count-adjusted fields separately. Use count-adjusted `sca.P.Value` and corresponding statistic/variance for the DEqMS endpoint, never mislabel original `P.Value` as DEqMS. Compute declared-family BH centrally from the correct raw P. Confirm exact columns and compatible contrast covariance in the pinned version. Effect-threshold testing is not TREAT unless officially and independently validated for the DEqMS model; reject unsupported hypotheses. [Official DEqMS vignette](https://bioconductor.org/packages/release/bioc/vignettes/DEqMS/inst/doc/DEqMS-package-vignette.html).

**proDA:** use documented proDA/test_diff APIs and normalization assumptions from the pinned version. Test coefficients/contrasts against zero or documented reduced-model comparisons; do not advertise TREAT, arbitrary random effects or classical limma df under this model. Export its native uncertainty/inference fields and dropout diagnostics with model-specific names. Any derived CI must use the backend's actual documented variance/distribution/df and pass an independent reference calculation; absent unsupported inference is explicitly unavailable rather than borrowed from a different engine. [proDA introduction](https://const-ae.github.io/proDA/articles/Introduction.html), [test_diff reference](https://const-ae.github.io/proDA/reference/test_diff.html).

## 7. Multiplicity and result semantics

A family is an explicit set of hypothesis rows identified before results are observed by primary/secondary status, endpoint type, planned contrasts and feature/set universe policy. Default primary protein family pools all declared primary zero-null contrast-feature tests in the selected primary model; threshold-null endpoints use their own declared primary family if they are the primary question. Secondary contrasts, method sensitivities, detection endpoints and pathway nulls have separate named families. Per-contrast families are permitted only when explicitly configured/reported; no study-wide claim is then allowed.

BH is applied once to finite raw P values in each family and NA rows retain failure/nonestimability reasons. Output records n_planned, n_eligible, n_tested_finite and adjustment method/assumptions. Numerical/runtime failure of an eligible planned test makes the family incomplete; finite-P adjustment cannot preserve an unqualified completed-family claim. Prespecified ineligibility is distinct. Do not turn a failed test into P=1 to conceal failure. If using an explicit larger denominator as a conservative planned-family policy, record it and test that definition; do not confuse it with R's default finite-P behavior. Dependence assumptions and hierarchical/repeated analyses remain scientific caveats. For optional Benjamini–Yekutieli, expose the different method explicitly; do not choose it after seeing significance.

Omnibus tests have their own endpoints/families and do not automatically license unadjusted post-hoc pairwise claims. A stage interaction is tested directly; one significant stage and one nonsignificant stage does not prove heterogeneity. Main report includes all planned primary and secondary contrasts, including the baseline Negr1 auxiliary contrast, even when only some generate discoveries.

## 8. Annotation and enrichment universes

Gene mapping consumes a local versioned snapshot with species/taxonomy, source database, release, retrieval date, identifier type, mapping method, one-to-many policy and hashes. Raw gene symbols are not a unique key across species. Ortholog projection is declared with source/target species and evidence; rat MSigDB sets are not silently called experimentally validated rat-native pathways. Mapping ambiguity, unmapped features, duplicate genes and membership losses are reported.

Default expression-gene construction for matrix-based pathway tests excludes ambiguous multi-gene protein groups and selects a deterministic representative per uniquely mapped gene using a **label-independent** rule (best overall observed coverage, then highest overall median abundance, then stable feature ID). Save the exact mapping and excluded rows. An explicit robust median aggregation is a sensitivity option with its scale/coverage and different estimand recorded. Never select the most significant protein per gene as the maintained default. Use the resulting gene expression matrix to fit its own documented gene-level model for pathways; do not mix a representative gene matrix with unrelated maximum-rank protein statistics.

Matrix-based pathway inference uses a finite gene universe across observations required by its design. No silent primary imputation to satisfy CAMERA/ROAST. If complete-row filtering yields inadequate overlap, mark the method inapplicable with coverage diagnostics or use an explicitly named exploratory method; do not switch nulls while retaining the same report label. For ORA, the universe is eligible tested mapped genes, not the whole genome. Filter sets by overlap size before testing (default 10–500; example sensitivity 5–500 for legacy comparison), retain zero-overlap tested outcomes where relevant, and adjust all eligible sets, not only significant/positive-overlap sets.

## 9. Pathway methods

**Independent linear designs:** primary correlation-aware competitive CAMERA on the frozen gene matrix/design and planned contrast, `inter.gene.cor=NA` to estimate correlation rather than assuming the default 0.01. Record estimates, null, gene universe and warnings. Confirm exact installed behavior for weights/nonorthogonal contrasts and validate against direct reference calls. CAMERA does not support the block/correlation arguments used by duplicateCorrelation and may disregard them; dispatch must reject this combination. [CAMERA source](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bioc/limma/master/R/geneset-camera.R).

**Blocked/repeated linear designs:** a fixed-subject paired model passes the subject-adjusted design. Only duplicateCorrelation-based random-block models additionally pass tested block/correlation arguments; never encode the same subject both ways. This is a self-contained rotation null, not CAMERA's competitive null. Directional and mixed outcomes are separate endpoints with separate declared families. Set `midp=FALSE` and compute declared-family adjustments from the appropriate raw directional/mixed P columns; retain native package fields for audit. Record nrot and seed (publication/release default at least 9,999 rotations; smaller smoke-test count labeled test-only), precision and attainable P resolution. ROAST requires finite numeric expression, not NA/Inf. [ROAST documentation](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bioc/limma/master/man/roast.Rd).

**Preranked fgsea:** optional exploratory weighted gene-set enrichment, not a biological-label permutation or independent replication. Use a declared ranking statistic: signed moderated t for eligible limma zero-null inference, or a documented engine-native zero-null statistic for other engines. Signed −log10(P) remains an explicitly named legacy sensitivity. Stable tie ordering, duplicate mapping, finite statistics and min/max set sizes are recorded. Preserve ES/NES/P/padj/log2err/leading-edge/size, warnings, seed and parallel backend. eps=0 is permitted but does not make P exact. fgsea adjusted values and centrally recomputed family q values are distinct columns.

**ORA:** default optional exploratory analysis of a prespecified tested-gene foreground, usually FDR-selected up/down separately with its own family. Raw-P candidate ORA must say raw-P exploratory. Implement exact hypergeometric tail against the tested universe, output overlap counts/membership and background, and validate against hand calculations. No foreground yields an explanatory empty foreground result; no discoveries must not crash reporting.

**Alternative engines:** DEqMS/proDA abundance inference does not automatically make CAMERA/ROAST an equivalent test of that engine. A linear complete-case gene model may be offered as a separately labeled sensitivity with its own eligibility/family; fgsea from engine-native ranks is exploratory. Never label this mismatch as confirmation of the primary engine. Collection retrieval fails before analysis if a required snapshot is missing; offline production does not quietly drop that collection.

## 10. Treatment response and valid inference

For a disease/control/treatment axis define d=U−C, t=T−U and r=T−C=d+t. Store all three estimates, intervals and model covariance. The joint stage interaction is a separate contrast. Shared U implies Cov(d,t)=−Var(U) under independent group-mean errors, so opposing effects are not independent evidence of rescue. Reports say association unless experimental evidence and estimand justify a causal interpretation; statistical software does not prove randomization.

Default response analysis is descriptive across a prespecified eligible feature universe. RI=−t/d is unavailable if the denominator is below a configured descriptive minimum or not estimable. Do not attach a naive CI to a ratio by dividing interval endpoints. A supported uncertainty option must use correct joint contrast covariance (e.g. Fieller or documented parametric simulation) and allow unbounded intervals; unsupported nonlinear inference is omitted with a reason. Selection by current raw disease P is permitted only as an explicitly exploratory display subset and cannot drive a confirmatory score test.

Descriptive categories use a declared tolerance epsilon in (0,1): RI<0 adverse-direction association; 0≤RI<1−epsilon partial movement toward control; 1−epsilon≤RI≤1+epsilon near-control effect ratio; RI>1+epsilon overshoot. Also flag RI>2 where |r|>|d| in the opposite direction. These are descriptive estimated categories, not “formal rescue”. The older 0.3/0.8 thresholds may be reproduced only in legacy comparison. A nonsignificant r cannot be named restoration or equivalence.

Optional practical equivalence tests use two one-sided tests against a prespecified residual-effect margin with valid model covariance and df. Export both one-sided P values, intersection-union P=max(P_lower,P_upper), confidence interval and family-adjusted result. With alpha=.05, the conventional TOST equivalence interval is 90%; label it distinctly from default 95% effect intervals. If a joint restoration claim also requires disease and opposing treatment evidence, define the full conjunction hypothesis before testing and adjust its valid combined intersection-union endpoint; do not intersect separately FDR-filtered lists and claim guaranteed rescue FDR. Limit initial inferential restoration mode to independently defined disease direction and eligible model-supported conjunction; otherwise provide component tests and descriptive interpretation.

**Independent score validation:** a score file must contain fixed feature IDs, weights/directions, training-data identity/hash, transforms and evidence that training/validation biological units and repeated-measures subjects are disjoint. Different injections or visits of the same subject do not establish independence. Apply frozen training centering/scaling, not refit normalization of the score to validation outcomes. For independent two-group data, exact absolute-tail randomization enumerates all admissible allocations and returns k/N, including the observed allocation once; randomized Monte Carlo includes the observed arrangement with the appropriate +1 rule and uncertainty. Repeated/paired designs restrict permutations to valid sign flips or within-block allocations with explicit null/exchangeability evidence. Floating tie tolerance is specified, tested and reported. If exchangeability is not justified, no permutation P value is produced.

The current-data selected-score analysis is **descriptive only** in this release. A fully nested label-reselection randomization engine is deliberately not exposed as inferential without a separately specified and validated randomization scheme accounting for disease selection. This avoids pretending a generic permutation wrapper solves selection bias. Store why testing is inapplicable; do not treat this scientific restriction as an unimplemented feature.

## 11. Claims and reporting

All reports derive status/counts from structured artifacts. Identify experimental n, tested universe, effect/hypothesis, primary versus sensitivity model, adjustment family, unknown metadata, input scale/imputation and external resource versions. Statistical significance cannot imply effect-size certainty, tissue origin, mechanism or raw-MS identification confidence. Print “no features meet the declared threshold” rather than “no biological effect”. Display nonsignificant and failed tests with their proper meanings. Cross-link every plot to exact source data; figures must not claim independent n equal to proteins when inference is at the animal level.

## 12. Mandatory scientific verification

Implementation must satisfy validation-strategy.md: direct-reference engine comparisons, sparse-design exactness, biological-unit resampling, finite-universe mapping, correct endpoint/family adjustment, selection-null/overshoot/exhaustive-permutation cases, no-discovery reporting and whole-run provenance. Reproducibility gates are distinct from statistical calibration. No one configuration can be promised “most scientifically sound” for all assays; scientifically explicit applicability and verified behavior are the product guarantee.
